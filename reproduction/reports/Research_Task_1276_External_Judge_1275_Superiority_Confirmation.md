# Research Task #1276: External Judge Confirmation of #1275 Superiority

Date: 2026-07-16 KST

## Question

Does the #1275 post-hoc superiority signal (selected > full, 44:6,
p=3.2e-08 under the Claude panel) survive an external judge family?

## Method

All 595 blinded #1275 judge requests re-judged by gpt-4.1-mini (temp 0,
JSON mode), unblinded with the task1275 sha scheme. Gates pre-registered
BEFORE the run: E1 non-inferiority (delta >= -0.05); E2 superiority
(discordant sign test p < 0.05 with selected ahead). Spend $0.099546
(ceiling $0.11), 0 failures.

## Result

```text
claim_decision: external_judge_confirms_1275_superiority
success: true (E1 and E2)
selected_semantic_correct_rate: 0.9681
full_semantic_correct_rate: 0.9092
delta: +0.0588
discordant: selected-only 43 vs full-only 8, sign test p = 6.9e-07
agreement with Claude 3-pass majority: 99.24% (1190 labels)
```

## Interpretation

1. The superiority finding now holds under TWO independent judge families
   on the same 595-pair sample:

```text
                    Claude panel     external gpt-4.1-mini
   selected rate    0.9697           0.9681
   full rate        0.9059           0.9092
   discordant       44 : 6           43 : 8
   sign test p      3.2e-08          6.9e-07
```

2. Judge agreement of 99.24% on 1190 labels also retro-validates the
   Claude-panel protocol used throughout the program.
3. Upgraded claim (C2+ in the evidence report): on this sample,
   named-entity-document selection does not merely preserve quality at 60%
   lower cost — it IMPROVES semantic accuracy by removing distractor-driven
   comparison errors, under both judge families.

## Remaining caveats (honest residue)

```text
1. Same-data confirmation: superiority was born post-hoc on these 595 pairs
   and confirmed on the SAME pairs with a different judge. The gold-standard
   closure is the untouched remaining 386 holdout pairs with superiority as
   the PRE-registered hypothesis (~$0.25 generation + ~$0.07 judge).
2. The external judge shares a family with the GENERATOR (gpt-4.1-mini).
   This affinity is symmetric across arms (both arms generated by the same
   model), so it cannot favor selected over full, but a third judge family
   would be cleaner still.
3. Single generation model; single dataset family for regime 2.
```
